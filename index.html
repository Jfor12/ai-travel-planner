How it works

AI Travel Planner

Pick a city and a month. It searches the web and writes a short briefing: what to eat, which neighbourhoods suit you, how to get around, the scams to avoid and what the weather will be like.

Written by AI from web research, so check anything important with official sources. Saved trips are visible to everyone. New briefings are limited to five an hour.
